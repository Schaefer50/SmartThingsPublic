## Pricing notes

- Launch at **$4.99** so the listing picks up its first sales and reviews, then raise it to **$6.99–$8.99**.
- Sell a **3-color bundle** at about $11.99.
- Etsy fees: $0.20 listing fee (renews every 4 months or after each sale), 6.5% transaction fee, and about 3% + $0.25 payment processing. At $6.99 you keep roughly $5.90 per sale. Hosting and printing cost nothing.
